BUILD & EDIT

Build: select a coloured dot and drag between stations. Extend a ringed end; join the two ends to make a loop.

Select: tap a station to cycle its lines. Tap empty ground to deselect. Tap a mode glyph to hide its lines.

Edit: tap track to bend it. Drag track onto a station to add a stop. Pull a middle stop away to remove its service and keep the track. Undo reverses your last edit.

Move: drag empty ground to pan; pinch to zoom. On desktop, scroll to pan, Control-scroll to zoom, F to centre, Space for speed.
